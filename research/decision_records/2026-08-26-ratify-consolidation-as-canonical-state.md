## What is ratified as settled

**native30.** The capability-floor result and the seed-noise/null conclusions
stand, **with the tokenizer/context qualification preserved**. The claim that the
wave ran cleanly under the anti-leakage gate is **not** made; it stays PENDING
until the gated `reval30_*_fixed_*` re-run exists.

**DMLA.** A **hypothesis family, not an architecture commitment**. The A0→A7
ladder is not approved as written. Multiscale representation, persistent state,
memory, iterative inference, sparse routing and physics-inspired dynamics are
**not assumed beneficial** until each has survived a controlled experiment.

**E-DELIMIT.** Complete, and **VOID for H5 — not a refutation**. A′ replicated
L000 exactly under the common analyzer. Arm B failed its retrieval-preservation
precondition, LOCATED falling 97/120 → 30/120, so the experiment cannot answer
whether improved delimitation follows from the manipulation with retrieval held
constant.

**R8 is methodology now, not an incident note.** Output-format *feasibility* is
not evidence that an intervention preserved the task. Any future manipulation
meant to isolate delimitation must explicitly demonstrate retrieval/task
invariance.

**Next span-port candidate.** The repaired two-stage design is correct: let the
model select the turn, then enumerate sub-spans only within *the turn it
selected*, conditioning delimitation on the model's own retrieval decision rather
than silently supplying the gold turn. **Not authorized.** Requires its own
pre-registration, decision rule, invariance requirement, and experiment-scoped
authorization.

**Scale.** Do not shrink to 5–10M for experimental convenience. A small-model
mechanism study must **first establish that the smaller regime can distinguish
the hypotheses being tested**; that demonstration is the prerequisite experiment.

**Product framing.** Nano vNext is specified around the product — a
general-purpose personal/medical intelligence system running primarily locally
and cheaply — not around DMLA.

**Architecture that survives whichever neural architecture wins.** The eleven
planes, ontology, epistemic contract, hybrid world model, WorkSlice cognition
model, deterministic substrate, capability fabric, artifact compiler, authority
boundaries, dependency/invalidation model, and seven-level learning hierarchy.

**Medical/personal use is a first-class constraint**, not a security wrapper. A
correct fact attached to the wrong patient is an intelligence failure as well as
a safety failure.

**History is preserved exactly.** No exploratory work is retroactively converted
into confirmatory evidence. The `ddb5ce6` authorization/procedural gap stays in
the record.

**Repository.** Preserve all experiment artifacts and branch provenance. **Do not
rewrite or merge active peer-session work merely to make the tree look cleaner.**

**Mac-first.** Reproducible Apple-Silicon/MPS-compatible experiments with explicit
compute, memory, latency and cost instrumentation. No infrastructure or
architectural complexity without a demonstrated experimental reason.

## What this record changed in the documents

Three things were genuinely new and are now in the spec:

1. **One minimum standard, twelve fields** (§20). The spec previously carried two
   divergent lists — a six-field chain in §20 and a nine-field list in §25. They
   are replaced by the single ratified chain: hypothesis → instrument → measured
   bottleneck → manipulation → invariance condition → baseline → expected benefit
   → cost → falsifier → preregistered decision rule → authorization → provenance.
2. **The readiness gate** (§25), five questions, with the consequence stated:
   *if they cannot be answered beforehand, the experiment is not ready.* Question
   4 — **what competing explanations does the experiment distinguish?** — is new
   to this program's standard and is what ranks candidates.
3. **The governing commitment** (Standing rule): *Nano vNext is not going to be
   designed by accumulating mechanisms. It will be discovered by discriminating
   experiments.*
