## Decision/revision ledger (append-only relationship)

1. **Q13 / native model-quality baseline — strengthened, not promoted.** Evidence `results/scale2/q13-verdict.json`, exact 386M run + benchmark above. Reason: strong native scaling and model-recipe progress; alternative explanations are parameter count, extra tokens, optimized data and Muon choices. Keep comparative architecture status **unselected**. The next cheap discriminant is an item-level context-use evaluation with a frozen prompt protocol.
2. **Q12/nested — “mixed” remains the registered verdict.** The 4×2 arm is a mechanism candidate; the 1×4 vs 4×1 pair and device results prevent general inference. No retrospective change to the L1–L3 predictions. New seeds are more informative than another interpretation of the same five.
3. **Q14 / systems — registered rejection for W1, with W2/W3 not executed.** The speed result does not support altering 64-wide heads; incomplete runs and unknown machine failure stay visible.
4. **G04 — supersession required.** PR #204 explicitly withdraws the earlier narrow-router claim after corrected code. Treat old values as invalid for that mechanism and cite its correction notice/results without deleting historical rows. The key remaining cheap test is a corrected, typed-vs-learned routing comparison on untouched streams.
5. **G06 — scoped experimental progress, not architectural selection.** Localized protection outperforms whole-history protection in a small bilinear world; result remains draft-branch evidence. Strongest alternatives are exact constrained writes, targeted replay, and established OWM/gradient-projection controls.
6. **Efficiency and scientific integrity:** distinguish source-reported output from independently replicated runs. Current repository notes estimate cloud charges but **billing was not audited**; this review launched no resources. Missing tokenizer/corpus/checkpoint digests in a result path are not filled by an attractive metric.

## Direct primary research considered (not Nano replications)

- **Allen-Zhu, “Physics of Language Models: Part 4.2 — Canon Layers at Scale,” arXiv:2611.24963v1, submitted Nov 12, 2026.** [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24963). Reports scale-dependent, tuned-architecture effects and severe in-context retrieval/two-hop blind spots despite standard benchmark scores. **Implication:** add cheap exact retrieval and two-hop context tests to Q13; consider token-mixing Canon layers as an explicitly costed comparator only if experiments justify it. This is not evidence for Nano Canon adoption.
- **Arefin et al., “Delayed Effects of Temporary Weight Decay in Muon Pretraining,” arXiv:2611.17876v1, submitted Nov 13, 2026.** [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17876). Author-reported delayed benefit from temporary decay across 162M–3.4B. **Implication:** do not judge Muon/decay solely from early checkpoints; a frozen final-budget test is required before substituting another schedule. Does not retroactively change Q13.
- **Li et al., “dLoop: Looped Diffusion Language Models via Recurrent Latent Refinement,” arXiv:2611.26275v1, submitted Nov 19, 2026.** [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26275). A parameter-shared latent refinement result for **diffusion** LMs, not automatically a causal autoregressive model. **Implication:** possible mechanistic comparator for Q12, but the diffusion/commitment and compute-accounting differences must be matched before claiming relevance to Nano's architecture.

These three papers are already public before the present review and are included as **newly verified context for the 2026-11-23 evidence assessment**, not represented as newly released on Nov 23. Abstract/method-level reported findings have not been independently replicated.
