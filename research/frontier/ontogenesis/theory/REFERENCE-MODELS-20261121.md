Where a 3:1 recurrent-to-attention reference is NOT appropriate:
1. As the starting reference for 10M to 500M parameter models trained on under 1B tokens. No source tests it there. The nearest ratio evidence (340M, 20B tokens) shows the ratio does not move language-model scores, and its '3-1' label is really 2:1. The published recipes use about 37 to 77 tokens per parameter, so under 1B tokens corresponds to roughly 13M to 27M parameters; the 32-block design is two orders of magnitude away.
2. As a quality prior at any scale. The only exact ablation has 3:1 ahead of 1:1 by 0.01 validation perplexity with no seeds, on a mixture-of-experts model trained with Muon; the authors prefer it for inference cost at long context.
3. For short-context training (2K to 4K tokens or less) under an equal-wall-clock rule. The recurrent kernel is 1.3x to 4.1x slower than FlashAttention-2 at 1K to 2K tokens on NVIDIA hardware and wins only from about 4K.
4. On Apple silicon or TPU with JAX. No optimised kernel is listed for either; the pure-PyTorch path was never timed. Mamba-3 kernels need Linux and an NVIDIA GPU.
5. In shallow models where the task needs several retrieval steps in one pass. At 4 to 8 blocks a 3:1 pattern leaves one or two full-attention layers; the two-hop study found one- and two-layer attention models fail in-context two-hop lookup, and the theory paper (worst case only) ties in-pass composition depth to the number of full-attention layers. A pure fixed-state model is far behind on extraction at 2K context (SWDE 28.5 vs 48.9), and the published hybrid that recovers it is 5:1 at 1.5B, not a shallow 3:1. This point is an inference across clusters and is untested.
6. As something SmolLM3, the Gated DeltaNet paper, arXiv 2507.06457, the shipped Kimi model or Mamba-3 can be cited for. Their patterns are, in order: positional encoding only; sliding-window hybrids; 2:1; 20:7; 5:1.
7. As a copy of the Qwen head configuration at small width. The recurrent mixer's key and value widths do not shrink with model width (at hidden 1024 it is as large as the FFN, and the vocabulary table is 33.8% of the model), while cutting head dim from 128 to 64 costs about one perplexity point at 400M.
