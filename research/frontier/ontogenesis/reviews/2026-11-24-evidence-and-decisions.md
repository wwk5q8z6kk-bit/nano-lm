## Newly relevant primary research
Lan et al., [On-Policy Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models (FlashMorph2), arXiv:2611.24809v1, 2026-11-18](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24809), [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.24809), abstract, introduction and related work inspected. Authors jointly adapt weights and sample discrete hybrid layouts; report gains over FlashMorph of 2.42 RULER, 1.08 LongBench and 0.76 recall-task points on Qwen3; selection cost 0.72–3.57 GPU-hours across 0.6B–8B. HALO replacement-based sensitivity is also prior art. This directly limits novelty and generality of Nano's frozen leave-one-out ranking; it may change after recovery. Cheapest comparator is rank stability before/after a bounded recovery using identical placement budget and held-out split, not immediate implementation of a new search system. External author-reported, not replicated here.
