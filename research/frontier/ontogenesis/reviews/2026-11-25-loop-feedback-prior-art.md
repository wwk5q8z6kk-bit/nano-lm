## New primary sources
1. Zhao et al., *Unlocking the Power of Loops in Pretrained LLMs with Loop Residuals*, [arXiv:2611.31094v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31094v2), first submitted Nov 23, revised Nov 24; [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31094v2), sections 2.3–2.4, 3.2–3.4, Tables 2/5 and limitations. Distinguish repeated states by structural loop/layer identity, and limit attention to recent loop outputs. Authors report a 29.1% relative math/code average improvement over SFT at twice its compute on Qwen3-1.7B. At fixed looping depth, their fused residual implementation adds 4.8% step time versus 54.9% without fusion (Table 2); this is not total loop overhead. SFT reasoning benchmarks on pretrained backbones do not establish gains for small from-scratch language models or correction.
2. Xiong et al., *Attention Latent Feedback*, [arXiv:2611.31154v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31154v1), submitted Nov 23; [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.31154v1), sections 3, 5, 6.2, 7 and Appendix A.3/B.1. Earlier token's deeper sublayer states feed current token's shallow layers, distinct from same-token depth mixing or repeated whole blocks. Authors report loss improvement of 0.022–0.024 nats and about 1.1% additional decoding FLOPs; fixed nearby-source feedback retains 89–94% of the reported gain. The extra training pass runs in the final quarter of the schedule, with estimated overall step-time cost 1.27x (late steps 2.07x); cache reads, prefill and memory traffic remain costs. Main comparisons have limited independent seeds, are on base MoE models, and do not demonstrate mobile latency or correction. FLOPs are not measured energy; nats are not BPB without a normalization conversion.

## Reconciliation and alternatives
- Preserve the Nov 23 ledger nested-reuse decision, scoped to the tested task. Canonical `research/frontier/ontogenesis/LEDGER.md` lines 248, 256 cite `results/nested-lr/verdict.json` and `PROTOCOL-NESTED-LR.md`: whole-block reuse positive on toy two-step chains; single-layer reuse indistinguishable; registered decision mixed. LoopRes is a distinct successor hypothesis for preserving iterative computation, not evidence that those old rules passed. Possible alternative explanations: SFT exposure, increased FLOPs per output, shortcut suppression rather than learned routing, or kernel effects.
- Preserve #203's D1 close at 14M and D2 reject unconditional coda, as recorded in `research/frontier/gate1_and_depth_routing/PROTOCOL.md`, `score_tournament.py` and `results/tournament/summary.json` at its pinned head. ALF is a different causal axis, so does not overturn D1/D2 or establish a value-residual mechanism. Alternative explanations include generic temporal recurrence, extra optimization compute, source dimensionality and train/evaluation unroll mismatch.
- Neither preprint is independently replicated here. Both warrant comparator/ablation design changes only. Do not combine mechanisms before identifying independent gains. The two papers are not substitutes for immutable source receipts, scorer fixes, or the #210/#211 P0 gates.

Measure held-out BPB and tasks, training and inference time, memory bytes and peak memory, prefill, and energy if instrumented; use both equal-token and equal-wall-clock comparisons with paired seed uncertainty. Escalate only if quality/compute or quality/energy Pareto gains survive and reproducibility is established.
