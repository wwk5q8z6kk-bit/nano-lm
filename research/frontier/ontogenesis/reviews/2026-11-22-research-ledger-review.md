## Decisions, interpretations and preservation

1. **Do not promote a novel architecture.** The strongest new evidence is for better *recipes* and more data at existing dense-model scales. Both are required comparators for any APS/AETHER, recurrent or new state-memory mechanism. A toy nested success is not a native language win.
2. **Do not change the original protocols, thresholds or negative results.** In particular, keep the V5 native replay primary H1 means (51.32% current-only, 44.71% fixed-total replay, 43.65% additive), the invalidated old 0.62 cache result, and the five-seed factorial F3/F6 failures. The newer 101M store result updates a pending status but does not retrospectively validate the old evaluator.
3. **Use explicit uncertainty and cost.** The relevant frontier is held-out quality and distinct acquisition/retention/transfer at fixed training time, total bytes and measured joules, not BPB at matched optimizer steps alone. Estimate noise on the same device before interpreting small deltas.
4. **Current canonical ledger is stale for several result families.** Its most recent operations section does not yet record the full Q10–Q12 outcomes, Pythia calibration, nested verdict, revised whole-entity damage or completed 101M leave-document-out result. This review is an additive candidate for append-only reconciliation, not an overwrite of older observations.

## Fresh, directly relevant primary papers to consider (not Nano replication)

- **Zeyuan Allen-Zhu, [Physics of Language Models, Part 4.2: Canon Layers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24963)** (arXiv v1 first submitted **2026-11-12**, surfaced in 2026-11-20 paper listings). Abstract reports 64 from-scratch pretraining runs and argues some architecture differences emerge only at very large token budgets; simple local mixing may narrow linear/Transformer gaps, yet in-context retrieval and two-hop reasoning remain weak. **Implication:** add context-retrieval/two-hop challenge sets and avoid a single headline leaderboard. Abstract-level check only; no Nano replication.
- **Arefin et al., [Delayed Effects of Temporary Weight Decay in Muon Pretraining](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17876)** (first submitted **2026-11-13**). Reported optimization gains can appear only late after decay is removed and depend on model scale and duration. **Implication:** evaluate optimizer and decay schedules at the final intended budget and log weight norms/relative-step trajectories, rather than promoting a short-run 14M recipe universally. Abstract-level check only.
- **Li et al., [dLoop: Looped Diffusion Language Models via Recurrent Latent Refinement](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26275)** (first submitted **2026-11-19**). Parameter-shared latent refinement before diffusion token commitment shows author-reported gains on reasoning/code tasks. **Implication:** a research comparator for adaptive latent computation, not evidence that causal autoregressive Nano LM should adopt diffusion or repetition. Abstract-level check only.
