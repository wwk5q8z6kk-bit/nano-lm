# Independent AI adjudication before inference

This is the third AI agent adjudication of two independent AI annotation passes over 24 synthetic encounters (120 fields). It is not human inter-annotator agreement, clinician review, clinical validation, or confirmatory evidence. All 120 field decisions were reviewed against the patient source and rubric.

## Inputs and blindness

Annotation inputs were limited to sources.json, annotation_rubric.md, annotations_a.json, annotations_b.json, annotation_a_process.md, and annotation_b_process.md in this directory. Required repository and machine instruction files were also read; an initial filename-only instruction lookup exposed no case or implementation content. No implementation, tests, historical cases or outcomes, inference outputs, model answers, product parser, or product scorer were inspected. The adjudicator saw both annotation sets, so this is adjudication, not a third blind annotation pass. Source strata were visible but were not evidence of patient facts.

The original source, rubric, annotation, and process files are retained unchanged. Their SHA-256 hashes are recorded in agreement.json. Outputs were authored before any inference outputs were seen by this adjudicator. A Python standard-library command serialized the decisions, compared all fields, and validated structure and exact patient evidence. Semantic decisions were made by the adjudicator, not by a parser or scorer.

Value normalization casefolds, collapses whitespace, and trims outer ASCII punctuation. It does not remove articles, possessives, or wrappers or make semantic substitutions. Thus equivalent textual values can disagree under this deliberately transparent lexical measure. Exact evidence means an order-independent set of resolved (start, end-exclusive, exact quote) triples, including punctuation and occurrence selection. Sentence splitting and extra spans are differences. Every one of the 120 comparisons, including not-applicable supported values, is present in agreement.json.

## Other reviewed boundaries

The corrected side and duration reports resolve the earlier alternatives; they are not conflicts. Activity-specific and symptom-specific severities remain U when one scalar cannot retain both. Explicitly uncertain strength does not erase a known medication identity. Family, coworker, notebook, and article statements are excluded from patient facts. The unresolved daily-ibuprofen versus no-ibuprofen-this-week pair remains C because the proposed week mix-up never supplies a resolved history. The compound medication/allergy question followed by one bare negative answer remains U for both target fields; the possible broad-denial reading is acknowledged. Clinician assertions are not promoted to patient reports.

For the pollen disagreement, U follows the explicit rubric inclusion of volunteered material allergies and the local allergy-answer context. This is a semantic report classification, not a medical diagnosis. A narrower interpretation of bothers as a mere trigger would yield A annotator's S; that interpretation limit is retained in the decision record.

For accepted fields without differences or shared overrides, the retained source-grounded rationale is in gold.json and the complete comparison is in agreement.json. No unsupported medical detail was added.

## Validation and limitations

- annotator_a: 24 cases, 120 fields; states {'S': 81, 'A': 20, 'M': 0, 'U': 18, 'C': 1}; 129 exact patient spans, 4 explicit-offset spans; zero structural or quote-validation errors.
- annotator_b: 24 cases, 120 fields; states {'S': 80, 'A': 20, 'M': 0, 'U': 19, 'C': 1}; 135 exact patient spans, 3 explicit-offset spans; zero structural or quote-validation errors.
- gold: 24 cases, 120 fields; states {'S': 80, 'A': 12, 'M': 0, 'U': 27, 'C': 1}; 134 exact patient spans, 4 explicit-offset spans; zero structural or quote-validation errors.

Validation checked exact case coverage; all five fields; permitted states and required keys; minimum evidence cardinalities; A restricted to MED/ALG; M having no evidence; nonempty rationales; exact transcript matching; patient-only turn boundaries; uniqueness for plain quotations; and correctness of every explicit zero-based offset. All repeated necessary gold quotations use explicit offsets. No product parser/scorer or repository tests were used.

These checks establish structural and citation integrity, not semantic truth or real-encounter generalization. This small synthetic cohort and AI-only process cannot establish human/clinician IAA. Original agreement metrics must remain distinct from adjudicator acceptance, and the eight shared rubric overrides are not independent A/B disagreements.
