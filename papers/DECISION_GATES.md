# Decision gates

These gates keep AI engineering, experiments, and scientific claims aligned
with Nano's mission. They are decision criteria, not a permission system.

Research passes through these gates to improve Nano: observe a failure, propose
a bounded training or engineering change, evaluate it against the current
checkpoint and relevant baselines, integrate a supported gain, and repeat.
Research artifacts and baselines are instruments in that loop, not independent
end states.

## G1 — Define the capability outcome

Pass when the work names:

- the Nano capability or failure being addressed;
- the input and output boundary;
- the metric and evaluation partition; and
- the smallest result that could change the AI roadmap.

Fail when the objective is simply to add a model, framework, benchmark, agent,
interface, or infrastructure layer.

## G2 — Establish the smallest sufficient baseline

Pass when the strongest honest deterministic, retrieval, or symbolic baseline
is implemented and measured under the same information and utility.

For extraction-like tasks, classical baselines are mandatory. They establish a
floor and localize residuals; they do not replace Nano's trained intelligence.
A training or architecture change should name the residual it intends to close.

## G3 — Define grounding, verification, and abstention

Pass when:

- consequential factual values bind to transcript evidence;
- unsupported and conflicting cases have explicit behavior;
- accuracy, coverage, abstention, latency, and cost are measured separately;
- raw learned-model behavior and post-verification behavior are reported and
  gated separately;
- thresholds and labels are fixed before the decision run; and
- private or restricted evaluation data remain isolated.

Fail when correctness depends on an unscoped model judge, post-rationalized
citation, or forced-answer behavior.

## G4 — Run a decision-relevant comparison

Pass when the comparison has frozen inputs, matched information, reproducible
artifacts, an explicit keep/kill rule, and enough power to change the next step.

One-off smoke tests and repository dogfood may validate engineering. They do not
become scientific evidence or representative AI capability validation.

## G5 — Promote an AI change

Promote a training, data, architecture, inference, grounding, or verification
change when it:

1. improves a held-out capability metric or closes a repeated measured failure;
2. beats the strongest cheaper alternative under matched utility;
3. preserves grounding, abstention, privacy, and regression behavior; and
4. has acceptable compute and maintenance cost.

Otherwise reject, narrow, or retain it only as evidence for Nano's next step.

### Integration requirement

A supported result enhances Nano only after it enters Nano's common inference
surface, passes the capability acceptance suite and relevant Fabric
regressions, updates the frozen baseline, and records the next unresolved
failure. A result that remains only in Wedge, Fabric, or experiment code has
informed Nano but has not yet improved Nano.

## G6 — Promote a scientific claim

Promotion into the Evidence Ledger requires a traceable protocol, code, inputs,
outputs, decision rule, limitations, and reproducibility status. Engineering
measurements remain engineering measurements until this evidence chain exists.

Negative results are retained. A new dataset, utility, verifier, or regime
creates a new claim; it does not erase a prior scoped failure.

## H5 decision and H6 admission

The frozen training-only selector chose H5 `seed-20260805-epoch-3` (SHA-256
`04ba7b4d0dc876ca3d8de7fe7d809ca16796e5bf55249ad93ba1dd3557c394fe`).

H5 is rejected. Its uncalibrated admission failure correctly left calibrated
raw, verifier-final, latency, and sealed-confirmation stages null. This is a
valid negative result for the exact mixture only. It does not prove replay
generally useless, Nano's architecture inadequate, or any residual mechanism
causal.

H5's component mismatch was 727 state-correct/span-wrong fields and 270
span-correct/state-wrong fields. Those aggregate counts motivate a bounded
diagnostic; they do not establish that state/evidence coupling caused H5's
errors or promise that H6 will improve them. The initial hidden-fusion concept
was rejected before freeze because it mixed a normalization change with a new
pointer-to-state-head gradient path, confounding interpretation and preventing
exact H5 functional identity at initialization.

The final H6 intervention changes only the existing boundary queries. It adds a
learned offset tensor shaped `[5 semantic states, 2 boundaries, 64 query
dimensions]`, exactly 640 parameters. For each field, the detached soft state
posterior selects separate start and end offsets, which enter as residuals on
the existing projected boundary queries. The offsets are zero-initialized, so
under the same seed H6 at step zero is functionally identical to a freshly
initialized H5; it does not resume a trained H5 checkpoint. Gold state is not
teacher-forced. H6 has 3,287,109 total parameters, including a 138,501-parameter
head.

H6 keeps byte-exact H5 fit and calibration data, the 50:50 replay mixture,
anchor, tokenizer, prompt, loss formulas, optimizer, seeds, batch size, three
epochs, 1,050 steps per seed, training-only selection, evaluator, verifier, and
all quality floors fixed. It may not add data, sweep replay ratios, change query
text, reweight loss, scale the trunk, or alter gates.

The preselected H6 candidate must meet every uncalibrated semantic, retention,
failure, and state-balance gate before calibration is allowed.

H6 stops at the first failed raw gate. Only a full raw pass may apply the one
training-only threshold and run calibrated and verifier-final quality. Both

Quality precedes matched latency, whose p50 remains capped at 1.2 times H1;
sealed confirmation remains inaccessible until both quality and latency pass.
If H6 fails, reject this exact coupling mechanism and return to diagnosis. Do
not automatically run a second architecture intervention.

## Operational use

Nano's AI capability contract is the
[Strategic Reset](STRATEGIC_RESET.md). Wedge's subordinate
[instrumentation contract](WEDGE_V1.md) governs its supporting document runtime;
Wedge work is admitted only through a Nano failure, transfer hypothesis, and
Nano acceptance gate. Work that passes these gates enters
[`EXECUTION_QUEUE.md`](EXECUTION_QUEUE.md) with a concrete acceptance test.
Additional process documents are unnecessary when the task, evidence, decision,
and next action fit in these canonical files.
