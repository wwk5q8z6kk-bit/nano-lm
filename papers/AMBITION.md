## Ambition question (locked framing)

E1 **KILL** answered a narrow question: under frozen old-task \(U\) on the closed
synthetic extraction world, generative is not the preferred substrate (M1 ≫ M0).

Ambition is **not** “build NanoScribe anyway.”

Ambition is to determine whether there exists a regime **R★** such that:

\[
U_{\mathrm{generative+verify}}(R★) > U_{\mathrm{classical}}(R★)
\]

under matched accuracy, coverage, review burden, latency, cost, implementation
complexity, and maintenance burden — with inclusion predicates that do **not**
cherry-pick by observing classical failure after the fact.
